# Section 2: Networking at Research Universities in the 1950s--1980s

## Learning Objectives

-   Understand how early academic networks used dial-up and leased lines
    for communication\
-   Explain store-and-forward networking and its economic tradeoffs\
-   Describe the invention and importance of packet switching\
-   Recognize how ARPANET research shaped the modern Internet

## 120-BB-02-02-Packets.txt

### Udemy Title

Packet Switching Explained: The Foundation of the Internet

### Udemy Description

This lecture introduces packet switching, the key innovation behind modern networking. You will learn how messages are broken into smaller packets, routed independently, and reassembled at their destination.  This approach allows efficient sharing of network resources and avoids the delays of earlier systems.

### YouTube Title

Packet Switching Explained with a Simple Postcard Example

### YouTube Description

What is packet switching?\
Using a simple postcard analogy, this lecture explains how data is
broken into pieces, routed across networks, and reassembled---forming
the backbone of the Internet.
