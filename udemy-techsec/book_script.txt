













I have written a book titled
“Introduction to Networking:
How the Internet Works”
that complements the material in
this Udemy course.

The book is available both in print
and free online.

It has been translated into
Spanish, Greek, and Russian.

One fun fact about the book
is that all of the figures are drawn
in a style called “Sketch Note”.

There are no words in the figures.
Only concepts.

That means every translation
can use the exact same artwork.

The book covers roughly
the first two-thirds of this course,
focusing on protocols, packets,
and layered network architecture.

It is designed to be an easy read
and a helpful companion
to the lectures and interviews
throughout the course.

I also created a simple
speech-to-text audiobook
that you can listen to with
any podcast application or
online.

I hope you find the book
a valuable addition to the class.

You will find a link
to the book’s web site
and podcast in the resources
associated with
this lecture.

Welcome to the course,
and I hope to see you online.

Cheers.
