










Welcome to
Internet Technology and Security
on Udemy.

My name is Charles Severance.

I am a Professor
at the University of Michigan
School of Information,

and I have been teaching
online programming
and technology courses
to millions of students
since 1997.

Why take a course
on Internet Technology
and Security?

Well, being a great programmer
is not just about programming.

You must also understand context.

In the age of AI,
the best programmers —
the master programmers —
understand the environment
in which software operates.

They know more
than language syntax.

They know more
than libraries.

They understand systems,
networks, history,
architecture,
and the human problems
that software is trying to solve.

I have created a series of courses
that provide a straightforward path
toward becoming
one of those master programmers.

But for me,
that path always starts
with history and context,

and always with an eye
toward the human side
of technology.

My courses on
Internet History,
Technology, and Security
provide a gentle introduction
to the technical details
of the Internet
and networked applications.

And don't worry —

there is no math,
and there is no programming.

Instead,
we focus on understanding ideas.

We learn about packets,
sockets, protocols,
and layered network architectures.

And we do it in a fun way.

I present the material
alongside my interviews
with many of the people
who actually helped build
the early Internet.

You get a sense
of the forty-year research effort
that led to the Internet
we all use today.

Frankly,
the course feels more like
a learning Netflix series.

Put it on your television.

Grab some snacks.

Sit back
and binge-watch lectures
and interviews
about how the Internet works.

There are no stressful deadlines.

No essays.

No pressure.

Just relax,
enjoy the material,
and learn.

My goal
is to start you on the path
toward becoming
a master programmer —

through enjoyable courses
where you learn a great deal
without making your head hurt.

At the end of the course,
we will talk again
about the road ahead
and the next steps
on your journey.

But for now,

just sit back,
relax,
and enjoy the course.

This course is about understanding
the Internet,
networking,
and how it all works.

Cheers,
and see you soon.
